# ROADMAP.md

# Employee Tracking SaaS - Project Roadmap

## Vision

Build a secure, scalable, multi-tenant Employee Tracking SaaS platform
for organizations that manage field employees. The platform should
support configurable tracking modes, real-time monitoring, analytics,
and enterprise-grade security.
